How to get a Suno song URL

Open the public song on Suno, then copy its URL from the browser address bar or Suno's share control. A supported link normally starts with suno.com/song/…, suno.com/s/… or suno.com/hook/…. Paste the complete link above rather than a song title or creator name.

Which Suno download fits your task?

The downloader reads one public Suno song or Hook link and shows only the files available for that item. Choose one file or use Download all to build a ZIP in your browser.

MP3, WAV or original audio

MP3 works in most players and includes available song information. Choose WAV when an editor needs uncompressed PCM, or choose original audio for the fastest path without transcoding. Decryption and optional conversion happen locally. A converted WAV provides editing compatibility; it cannot restore detail already missing from the source audio of your AI-generated song. Downloading on desktop and mobile

Desktop browser

On a desktop browser the file saves straight to your Downloads folder. Larger songs and ZIP bundles may take a moment while your browser fetches the asset and, for audio, decrypts and optionally converts it locally.

Why a download may open in a new tab

Some browsers hand certain media types to a built-in player instead of saving them. If that happens, use the player's own save or download control, or right-click the link and choose “Save as”. This is normal browser behavior and does not affect the file you receive.

Official Suno downloads and this public-link tool

Suno offers its own in-app download for songs you created. This tool is an independent, unofficial companion that reads a public share link you already have access to. Use the official route when it fits; use this tool when you need a quick local copy of a public link without signing in.

What you can download and how you can use it

You can download a public song or Hook you own or have permission to save. Keep personal backups, move files between devices, or feed editable formats into your own projects. Respect creator rights and Suno's terms when you share anything further.

Current limits and unsupported formats

The tool shows only files Suno exposes for a given public link. Private or removed songs, region-restricted items, and formats Suno does not publish will appear as unavailable. Very large playlists may be better handled by the batch or playlist downloader.

Privacy

Your link is processed in your browser. Where a fetch proxy is configured, only the minimum request needed to reach Suno is sent; song IDs and links are not stored or sent to analytics beyond anonymized, aggregated counts.

Respectful use

Download only what you own or are allowed to save. Don't redistribute other creators' work without permission, and don't use the tool to circumvent Suno's own limits. The tool is for personal backup and authorized workflows.
